ColorQuest Kids Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

ColorQuest Kids is published by Brecksville Labs and is designed for children ages 1–12 and their families. The app does not require an account, email address, phone number, full birthday, advertising identifier, or payment information.

Information stored on the device

A parent or child may create a local profile containing a first name or nickname, age, and selected avatar. ColorQuest also stores activity progress, resume positions, voluntary “I liked this” choices, drawing drafts, read-aloud preferences, and artwork. This information is used only on the device to restore work and suggest related learning paths.

Brecksville Labs does not operate a ColorQuest account, profile, analytics, advertising, or cloud-storage server, and the app does not send this local profile information to Brecksville Labs. Parents can edit or delete profiles, progress, and artwork through Parent Corner. Removing the app or clearing its storage also removes locally stored information.

Artwork export

Artwork stays on the device unless a parent chooses Download, Print, or Share. On Android, an exported picture is temporarily written to the application's private cache and passed to the Android system share sheet. The parent chooses the receiving application or destination. Once shared, the receiving service's privacy practices apply.

Read-aloud

ColorQuest can pass lesson text to the text-to-speech service supplied by the device or browser. ColorQuest does not record audio, request microphone access, receive the spoken text on a Brecksville Labs server, or retain speech-service results. Voice availability and processing depend on the selected device or browser service.

Internet access and external resources

Core activities work offline after the web app has cached its application shell, and the Android app bundles its core content locally. Discovery Lab does not perform live image-search or encyclopedia requests.

Math and Science lessons may offer a small set of manually curated links to official resources from NASA, NOAA, and PhET. These links appear behind a grown-up check and open outside the core app experience. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and a parent should supervise their use.

Data we do not collect or share

ColorQuest Kids does not include advertising, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, social profiles, chat, location collection, camera access, microphone access, contact access, or broad photo or storage access. Brecksville Labs does not sell or share children's personal information.

Parent controls and security

Parent Corner uses a randomized grown-up multiplication check before profile editing, artwork export, printing, or deletion. This is a practical child deterrent, not identity verification. A parent or guardian should supervise device sharing, external learning links, and Android's system share sheet.

Children's privacy

ColorQuest is intentionally designed to minimize data collection. Brecksville Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through ColorQuest. If a parent believes a child has sent personal information to Brecksville Labs outside the app, the parent may contact us to request review or deletion.

Contact

Parents and guardians may contact Brecksville Labs privately at brecksvillelabs@gmail.com. Please do not send a child's artwork, full name, school, address, or other unnecessary personal information.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
